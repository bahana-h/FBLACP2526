How to Use Chrysalis Connect

Explore Businesses

Click Explore or enter a location to browse local businesses. Use the search bar, category filter, and sort options to find what you need. Click any business card to see full details, reviews, and deals.

Shared Reviews

Click Shared Reviews in the nav and enter your backend URL (e.g. your deployed Chrysalis Connect Flask app) to sync reviews across devices. Leave blank to use reviews only on this device.

Save Favorites

On any business card or detail view, click the heart to add it to Favorites. View your list anytime via Favorites in the nav.

Leave Reviews

Open a business and scroll to Add a Review. Choose a rating (1–5 stars), write your comment, and solve the quick math verification. Reviews are saved locally and optionally to your Shared Reviews backend.

Add a Business

Know a local business that isn’t listed? Click Add Business, fill in the details, and solve the verification. New businesses appear in the directory right away.

Recommendations

Click Recommendations in the nav for top-rated and trending businesses based on the current list.

Map

Click Map in the nav to see all businesses that have location data on an interactive map. Click a marker to view details and open the business.

Q&A

Open Q&A in the nav to ask questions about Chrysalis Connect. Type your question and get instant answers from our help content.

Reports

Open Reports to see a presentable summary of your directory: total businesses, reviews, ratings, and a table you can filter and sort. Print or download the report for your records.
