How to Use Chrysalis Connect

Explore Businesses

Click Explore to browse all local businesses. Use the search bar, category filter, and sort options to find what you need. Click any business card to see full details, reviews, and deals.

Enter Your Name

Enter your name in the top-right nav and click Go to unlock favorites, reviews, and personalized recommendations. Your name is stored in your session only.

Save Favorites

On any business detail page, click Add to Favorites to save it to your list. View your favorites anytime under Browse → My Favorites.

Leave Reviews

Open a business and scroll to Leave a Review. Choose a rating (1–5 stars), write your comment, and solve the quick math verification. Reviews are saved permanently.

Add a Business

Know a local business that isn’t listed? Click Add Business, fill in the details, and solve the verification. New businesses appear in the directory right away.

Recommendations

Under Browse → Recommendations, get personalized picks based on your favorite businesses. The more you favorite, the smarter the suggestions.

Map

Click Map in the nav to see all local businesses on an interactive map. Click a marker to view details and go to the business page. Businesses with coordinates are shown on the map.
